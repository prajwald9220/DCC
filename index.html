About Us

Homraj B. Deshmukh — 25+ Years of Honest Construction.

I am Homraj B. Deshmukh, based in Nagpur and I have been working in the construction industry for over 25 years under the name Deshmukh Civil Construction. With more than 25 years of experience and over 50 successfully completed projects, I specialize in new home construction, renovation and repair work.

My approach is built on transparent estimates, personal supervision and complete honesty. I personally visit every site, carefully inspect every detail and make sure that every project is completed to the highest standards. Construction is not just my profession, it is my identity and reputation.

Whether you are planning to build a new home or renovate and repair your existing property, you can count on my experience and personal involvement. If you are planning your dream home or a renovation project, feel free to call me. I will personally meet with you, understand your requirements and guide you through every step of the process.

  • 25+ Years Experience: Trusted contractor serving Nagpur and nearby areas.
  • 50+ Completed Projects: 1BHK to 4BHK homes, multi-storey buildings, renovation and repair work.
  • Personal Site Supervision: I personally visit every site and inspect every detail.
  • Transparent Estimates: Clear rates, honest material usage, no hidden charges.
